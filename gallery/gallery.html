The National Research Council publishes them after the CBHCC approves changes. Each province or territory decides whether, when and how to adopt an edition, with any variations. Safety codes officers, working for an accredited organization, apply the adopted code.

Tabs

<wa-tab-group>Docs ↗

National Provincial Local Model codes are developed nationally through a consensus process and published by NRC. Alberta adopts an edition by regulation under the Safety Codes Act, sometimes with Alberta-specific changes. Accredited municipalities and agencies administer permits and inspections under the adopted code.
