Worked example · Situation

“Can we use the newest model code?”

A permit submission cites a newly published National Building Code. Does publication make that edition applicable?

  1. Publication ≠ adoption

    A new model code does not automatically become law in a province or territory.

  2. Confirm what is in force

    Check legislation and regulations for the adopted edition, amendments, effective dates and transition provisions.

  3. Document the basis

    Record the requirements and source. Resolve uncertainty with the authority having jurisdiction before assessing the design.

Conclusion

No. Confirm the applicable requirements first. Then assess the submission against them.
