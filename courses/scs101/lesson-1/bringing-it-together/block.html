• A code sets a minimum threshold

    Once adopted into law, a code establishes minimum enforceable requirements. It does not eliminate all risk.

  • Developed nationally, adopted provincially or territorially

    The national model codes provide a common technical foundation. Provinces and territories adopt them into law, sometimes with changes.

  • Requirements serve stated objectives

    Every technical requirement supports at least one of the five objectives: safety, health, accessibility, fire and structural protection of buildings, or environment.
