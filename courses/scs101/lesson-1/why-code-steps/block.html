1. 01

    The Blind Spot

    Builders use common practices of the era with unrecognized hazards, such as flammable materials or inward-swinging exit doors.

  2. 02

    The Incident

    A fire or collapse exposes the consequences of those hazards.

  3. 03

    The Forensic Lesson

    Investigators identify the failures and contributing factors.

  4. 04

    The Codification

    The findings inform new code requirements intended to prevent similar failures.
