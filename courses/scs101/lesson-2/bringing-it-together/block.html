• Technical codes can come from different sources

    Some disciplines use national model codes, while others use technical codes developed by standards development organizations. Both can establish the main technical requirements for a discipline.

  • Codes and standards work together

    A technical code may reference other standards for specific requirements, such as product performance, testing, installation, inspection, or maintenance. Those standards provide additional technical detail without replacing the code that references them.

  • Publication does not make a document law

    Codes and standards are developed and published through technical organizations and processes. Government adoption or incorporation into law determines which requirements have legal force.
